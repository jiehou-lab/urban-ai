## How the course is staffed

Each cohort runs with two faculty leads and two graduate teaching assistants. TA effort per cohort is budgeted
at roughly 32 hours: eight hours of live teaching support, eight hours of assignment review and grading, and
sixteen hours of slide and material preparation shared across two students.

Future cohorts will add **student facilitators** drawn from completing students, who help refine materials,
update cases from cohort feedback, and provide peer mentorship. That is how the experience scales to 10–12
cohorts a year without redeveloping it each time.

## Support

The School of Planning, Design and Construction has committed to sustaining Urban AI for at least three
academic years. The experience is developed with instructional design and accessibility support and is
structured along Quality Matters and Universal Design for Learning principles.
