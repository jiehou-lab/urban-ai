## Attendance and completion

Attend at least 7 of 8 meetings, submit all six deliverables, and keep the AI Use Log current. Completion earns
a non-credit certificate and a Spartan Experience Record entry. Weekly mini-tasks are graded
complete/incomplete by the TA and returned before the next meeting.
